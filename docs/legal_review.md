# Teaching alignment and legal review

Legal review pending.

The inventory, limited logs, explicit purposes and proposed retention periods are teaching controls informed by the [ODPC principles and rights guidance](https://www.odpc.go.ke/rights-of-a-data-subject/). These artefacts do not establish a lawful basis by themselves. Access and erasure requests require identity and authority verification; erasure is subject to applicable legal grounds and retention duties.

Health information needs additional safeguards, as described in the [ODPC health data guidance](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ODPC-Guidance-Note-on-Processing-of-Health-Data.pdf). Before live use, obtain legal assessment of sensitive-data conditions, notices, any registration duties, impact assessment, international transfers, automated recommendations, incident reporting and processors. No regulator approval, certification or compliance number is claimed.

The rights workflow is an operator teaching example; it does not automatically determine whether a request must be granted. Deletion evidence is retained separately and is never swept by the provided job. Its production retention and access rules require legal review.
